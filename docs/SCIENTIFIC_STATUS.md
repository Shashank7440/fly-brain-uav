# Scientific status and claims

## Supported by supplied artifacts
- Two main V8 protocols, 36 runs each, with saved results and checkpoints where provided.
- Weighted validation tables reproduce the saved JSON metrics and confusion matrices within rounding precision.
- Internal M2 → G1 → saved biological graph consistency.
- Exact rewired in/out-degree preservation and matching node/edge counts across graph controls.
- Recorded parameter counts: graph models 69,080; comparable-budget CNN 83,971.

## Not established
- A statistically reliable biological-connectivity advantage, equivalence, or causal topology-only benefit.
- New independent-route generalization from three training seeds on the same split.
- Raw-public-source authentication until the original inputs are recovered/hash-checked and extraction is rerun.
- Runtime/energy superiority, event-driven computation, physiological equivalence, closed-loop safety, or deployment readiness.

## Follow-up plan
Freeze a protocol before new evaluation. Prefer genuinely unused routes with trajectory/content separation and verified labels/alignment. Report per-route metrics/supports and retain the graph-realization/seed hierarchy. If new routes are unavailable, route-grouped outer cross-validation is a follow-up study requiring additional training, not pristine external validation. Do not describe the already inspected original test set as a fresh untouched holdout for weighted-protocol development.

A reasonable current interpretation is that biological connectivity is competitive with the tested controls, but its small weighted mean validation macro-F1 lead does not establish reliable superiority.
